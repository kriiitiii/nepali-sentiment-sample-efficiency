# Privacy and Ethics

- Data: public tweets collected and annotated by Sitaula et al. (2021). The released text contains no user handles, URLs or tweet IDs. NepSent adds no identifiers and collects no new data.
- Redistribution: no tweet text or label file is redistributed (see `data_source_audit.md`). The report does not quote or paraphrase individual tweets.
- Labels: the gold labels are the dataset authors' majority-vote annotations, and no agreement statistic was published for them. NepSent does not relabel data, and no language model was used to produce or alter labels.
- Content: tweets about illness and death during the COVID-19 pandemic. The data are used only for aggregate model evaluation.
- Intended use of results: methodological evidence about sample efficiency for Nepali text classification. Models trained here are not suitable for monitoring individuals or for public-health decisions.
- Model licenses: NepBERTa is non-commercial (CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0), and no fine-tuned weights are released.
